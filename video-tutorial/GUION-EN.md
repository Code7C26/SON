# Voice script — SON tutorial (English)

Translation of the Spanish voiceover, block by block, so each line lands on the same screen.
Leave a short pause (half a second) between paragraphs.

---

SON, step by step. Your whole ecosystem in one video tutorial.

It all starts in the web panel. As soon as you log in, you see your operation live: today's orders and every technician on the map.

In Work Orders you get the whole week, with the status of every visit.

To schedule a visit, click New Order. Choose the technician, enter the client, the address and the time, and write the checklist tasks.

With Assign Job, the order goes onto the calendar… and reaches the technician's phone.

Martín sees his day, opens the order and taps Start Job.

SON records a GPS check-in: proof that he was on site, and the visit timer starts.

First, the checklist: he ticks off each task as he completes it.

Then he adds photo evidence, straight from the camera.

In Notes, he records his observations.

Finally, the technician and the client's representative both sign, on the same screen. He taps Finish, and that's it.

At that moment, SON builds the PDF report with the tasks, the photos and the signatures, and emails it to the client.

The client receives it along with a survey, and rates the visit with a single tap.

Back in the panel, the order shows as completed, the report is saved in the client's folder, and in Analytics you see each technician's performance and your clients' satisfaction.

SON. Your whole operation, in one place.
